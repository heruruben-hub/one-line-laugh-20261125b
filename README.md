This is my entry for the make.fun bounty "One Line, One Laugh".

The challenge is simple: make someone laugh with a single line, and nothing more.

My line imagines a smart fridge that has had enough of its owner and takes the leftovers dispute to court.

The joke flips the usual script, so the appliance becomes the responsible adult while the human gets called an unfit snacker.

Running `node main.ts` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`.
